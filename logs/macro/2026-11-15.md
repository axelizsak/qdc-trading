### fed_us_rates (+0.60, confidence 0.70)

Waller floats 50bp, Logan wants two or more hikes, and Deutsche Bank sees hikes in January and March. MOVE is elevated, and the Fed meets on 8-9 December. Futures price three hikes by March, so the direction is hawkish but much is already priced.

Counter-argument: A December hike is nearly fully priced, and Bowman is more cautious. Softer November jobs data on 4 December or a lower PCE could pull yields back after a 37bp weekly jump.

### inflation (+0.40, confidence 0.55)

Vanguard raised its core forecast, and the BoE says more hikes are needed. Gas storage is low. Oil's fall of about 16% and market inflation expectations (5y5y flat at 2.17%) cap the upside.

Counter-argument: Brent has dropped sharply from $114 to about $96, which should ease energy inflation. Forward inflation expectations are anchored, and the October PPI was slightly below forecast.

### growth_recession (+0.20, confidence 0.45)
