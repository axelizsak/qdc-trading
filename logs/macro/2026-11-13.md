### fed_us_rates (+0.50, confidence 0.60)

October CPI beat forecasts for a third straight month and payrolls were strong. Waller and Logan favour hikes, and nine of 10 officials who spoke lean toward tightening. A December hike is fully priced and three hikes are priced by March. The 2-year yield rose to 4.73% and MOVE is at the 89th percentile. The 9 Dec FOMC with new dot plot could push the path higher.

Counter-argument: A December hike is already 100% priced. Doves such as Bowman, Williams and Barkin want less haste, and a 50bp move looks unlikely. The AI-led equity selloff and falling oil could soften the hawkish tone.

### inflation (+0.30, confidence 0.50)

Counter-argument: Brent fell about 20% in November and the 5y5y inflation expectation is flat at 2.17%. Container freight and copper are weak, so headline pressure may ease soon.
