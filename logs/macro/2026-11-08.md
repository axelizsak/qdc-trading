### ecb_rates (+0.35, confidence 0.65)

Lagarde hinted at a December hike and Nagel said one is probably needed. Euro-area inflation is 4.0% (a three-year high), and €STR futures price a 2.75% deposit rate on 17 December at about 79%. Most of this is already priced, so only a modest further hawkish drift is expected.

Counter-argument: Brent fell about 15% in a week, officials call underlying inflation and wages stable, and the hawkish outcome is largely priced in. A tech-led risk-off could soften the tone.

### fed_us_rates (+0.30, confidence 0.60)

Hawkish Fed speakers (Logan, Hammack, Musalem) and strong October payrolls (+165k) keep a December hike likely. CME FedWatch shows about 70% and Polymarket about 76% for another 2026 hike. The 2-year yield is 4.5%, and Polymarket puts a 2026 cut at about 2%.

Counter-argument: Hike odds fell from 83% to 70% during the week, and 5-year breakevens dropped 20 bp. The US CPI print on 10 November is expected to be soft, and the equity rout could temper the Fed.

### inflation (+0.10, confidence 0.35)

Euro-area inflation is 4.0% with energy at 20.8%, and central banks are tightening. However, the oil drop and falling breakevens limit further acceleration in the near term, so the near-term signal is only slightly positive.

Counter-argument: Brent -15%, TTF -15% and lower breakevens point to cooling. US October CPI is forecast at about 0.2-0.3% m/m with freight rates flat, which argues for a negative direction.

### growth_recession (+0.10, confidence 0.40)

Counter-argument: The Nasdaq is in correction, the AI capex narrative is cracking, and rate hikes from both the Fed and the ECB are a headwind. UK and euro retail data are weak, and the copper/gold ratio is falling.

### eurusd (+0.10, confidence 0.30)

Counter-argument: Strong US payrolls, a hawkish Fed, safe-haven dollar demand during the tech selloff, high European energy costs and French debt worries all favour a weaker euro.

### oil (+0.10, confidence 0.25)

### gold_silver (+0.20, confidence 0.35)
