### ecb_rates (+0.50, confidence 0.60)

Lagarde said only major shifts would stop a 25bp December hike, and Nagel and Knot back it. Euro-area headline inflation is 4.0% and core 2.5%. The decision is on 17 Dec, inside the window, and markets already price much of it, so extra upside is limited. Weak PMI is a minor offset.

Counter-argument: The hike is largely priced in, so the surprise is small. Falling oil and TTF plus a four-month PMI low could soften the tone. Lagarde said wage indicators show no alarming spiral.

### fed_us_rates (+0.40, confidence 0.55)

Strong October payrolls (+165K vs ~65K) and unemployment of 4.1% lifted December hike odds to 73-80%. Polymarket shows 98% for no cuts and 76% for another hike. JPM forecasts a December hike. The 10 Nov CPI is a key risk event.

Counter-argument: Warsh is seen as neutral to dovish and declined to hike in October. Breakeven inflation fell, oil collapsed, and the midterm risk-off move cut hike odds earlier in the week. Much of the hike is priced.

### inflation (+0.20, confidence 0.40)

Counter-argument: Brent -16% on the week and TTF -15% feed through to lower headline prints and breakevens. Core is stable at 2.5%.

### growth_recession (+0.00, confidence 0.30)
