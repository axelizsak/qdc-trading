### ecb_rates (+0.50, confidence 0.60)

Lane said a December hike would be prudent if the data hold. Lagarde said it would take 'significant changes' to deter a hike on 17 December. The German and Italian central bank heads have also signalled a move. Eurozone inflation is at 4.0% with core at 2.5%. Counter-argument: A December hike is already the consensus. A cooling in energy prices or the French fiscal and political drag could temper the guidance for 2027.

### fed_us_rates (+0.45, confidence 0.65)

October CPI was hotter than expected and payrolls were strong. Waller leans toward 25bp and says 50bp should be considered, and Logan sees two more hikes. Futures price about 29bp for 8-9 December. Polymarket gives 96% for another hike in 2026. The 2-year yield is about 4.74% and the 10-year about 5%. Direction is positive but partly priced in.

Counter-argument: The hike is already priced in, and Bowman flags financial-market risks. Equity weakness and a possible peak in AI-driven inflation could soften the Fed's tone. The 30-year auction on 12 November is a wildcard.

### inflation (+0.45, confidence 0.55)

US October CPI was +0.4% m/m against 0.3% expected, and core was +0.3% for a third straight month. Euro-area flash inflation rose to 4.0% with energy at 20.8%. Lagarde expects inflation to stay high. EU gas storage is very low. Brent is off its $117 peak, which limits further upside.

Counter-argument: Brent fell about 16% in a week and TTF fell 15%, so energy inflation may roll over. 5y5y inflation expectations eased to 2.17%, and Bloomberg Economics says AI-driven inflation has peaked.

### growth_recession (+0.20, confidence 0.40)

GDPNow puts Q4 at 3.5% and Polymarket gives only 2% odds of a US recession. Korean exports are at records, TSMC is strong and the labour market is solid. France is set to avoid a technical recession. Hawkish central banks and the AI-related equity correction cap the upside.

Counter-argument: Aggressive hikes, a Nasdaq correction, soft Chinese domestic demand and weak copper could signal fragility. T. Rowe warns the Fed could hike until something breaks.

### energy_weather (+0.10, confidence 0.30)

### china_em (+0.15, confidence 0.30)

Counter-argument: The export-led surplus signals imbalance and tariff risk stays elevated into 2027. Rising US yields near 5% and a strong dollar historically hurt EM assets.
